Thankful for the Gospel

Romans 1:16 (KJV)

“For I am not ashamed of the Gospel of Christ: for it is the power of God unto salvation to every one that believeth; to the Jew first, and also to the Greek.”

Paul wrote Romans around A.D. 57 during his third missionary journey. Though he had not yet visited the believers in Rome, he longed to encourage them with the truth of the Gospel that had transformed his own life.

The word Gospel comes from the Greek εὐαγγέλιον (euangelion), meaning “good news.” The word power comes from δύναμις (dynamis), meaning “power” or “strength.” Paul reminds believers that the Gospel is not merely good advice; it is God’s power at work to save and transform lives.

As many believers reflect on thanksgiving during this season, the Gospel remains our greatest reason for gratitude. Before we thank God for daily blessings, provisions, and opportunities, we thank Him for His gift of salvation through Jesus Christ. Every blessing of the Christian life flows from this good news.

Today, thank God for the Gospel. The same Gospel that changed Paul’s life continues to display God’s power in all who believe.

Prayer

Heavenly Father, thank You for the Gospel of Jesus Christ. Thank You for Your saving power and for the hope You have given through Your Son. Fill my heart with gratitude and help me live each day in a manner worthy of the Gospel. In Jesus’ Name. Amen.
